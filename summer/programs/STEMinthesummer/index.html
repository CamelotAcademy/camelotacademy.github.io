STEM in the summer

Register

Ages 12-18
Dates/Times Full Day: 07/06/2026-07/10/2026 (Monday-Friday) from 8:30 AM - 3:30 PM
Price Camelot Families - $365
General Public - $375
Registration period Registration for this schedule starts on 01/12/2026 and ends on 06/01/2026
Restrictions Participants must currently be 12 years to 18 years old.
In-person location Camelot Academy: 809 Proctor St, Durham, NC 27707, USA
Notes There is no before or after care options available

This program will be led by one of our Math teachers, Bev.

For further information or questions please contact shields@camelotacademy.org

Program Locations: Camelot Academy and other locations on the Eno River will be used during this program. Transportation between the Academy and field trip destinations will be provided using school vans and buses. Additional details about these destinations will be shared as the start of your Summer Program approaches.
Description Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math are cornerstones of understanding our universe. In this five-day program, students curious about STEM will investigate the many ways that these subjects complement each other through hands-on experiences in biology, chemistry, physics, technology, and engineering problem-solving, all while reinforcing their math skills through real-life applications.

NOTE: Registered students will be contacted with a required survey prior to the start of the program so that activities can be better tailored to their individual needs and interests.
