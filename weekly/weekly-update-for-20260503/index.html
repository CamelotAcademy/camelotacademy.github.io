ANNOUNCE:

Tuesday is National Teacher Appreciation Day!  Your cards and notes are always treasured keepsakes for the Team. If the time feels right, please consider taking a few moments to thank an educator - at Camelot or in the outside world - who has had a positive impact. 

 

The Class of ‘26 looks forward to sharing their graduation with the entire Camelot Family on Friday, May 22 at the Carolina Theater.  THIS IS A WHOLE SCHOOL EVENT WITH ALL STUDENTS PARTICIPATING!  The event begins at 5 PM in the beautiful Fletcher Hall.  We have some special things planned - this is a one-of-a-kind graduation!!!

 

On Monday at pick-up we will have eggs and five more Mother’s Day whipped honey boxes to meet the demand (they sold out Friday night).  If you arrive later in the afternoon, please email me to set eggs or a honey box aside.

In addition to the ongoing respiratory illnesses, last week we had several staff and students hit with GI distress.  Please keep students with symptoms home for AT LEAST 24 hours symptom-free.

 

I hope you have a great week!
