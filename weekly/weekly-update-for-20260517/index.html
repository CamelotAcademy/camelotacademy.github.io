Weekly Update for 05/17/2026

Sunday, May 17, 2026

Greetings and Happy Last Week of the School Year!

 

Welcome, Colin Van Duyne - the newest addition to the Faculty Kids Club.  Congratulations to Teacher Thomas, his wife Megan and Big Sister Gwen on the arrival of Colin who was born Friday, May15.

 

 

BEHIND THE SCENES:

One of my very most cherished annual moments happened last week - my time with the seniors when they read their graduation speeches to each other.  It never fails to be a poignant gathering and one can sense the reality of where we are in time and space wash over the soon-to-be alums.  This year was especially touching - there were a lot of tears- for all of us.  I was also struck by how even in this group of young people, many of whom have known each other for a decade or more, these heartfelt words of farewell opened new vistas to understanding each other. We are excited to share these special messages with you at Friday’s graduation ceremony. 

 

REMIND:

Upper School Students should consult their Exam Review Sheets to know what material will be covered on their exams this week.

 

Upper School Students need to return ALL TEXTBOOKS and Summer Reading Forms by Tuesday.

 

Monday

- Upper School Language exams

- Gold Students who do not take a Language class - or seniors exempt from their Language exam - should not attend school during the exam period (8:45-11:45 AM). They should arrive at 11:45.

   - Seniors transported to/from Yates for speech rehearsal with Magda 12:15-3:30.

 

Tuesday

- Upper School Math exams

            - Seniors who are exempt from their math exam should arrive at 11:45 for afternoon classes.

 

Wednesday

- Last Day on Campus - Whole School graduation rehearsal, yearbooks and fun! (Including pizza and cake!)

- 10 AM- Seniors arrive for speech rehearsal (this is a slightly later time than announced last week - we already took the senior pictures)

 

Thursday

Friday

- GRADUATION at the Carolina Theater!

This is a K-12 PARENT REQUIRED EVENT!  (5-7 PM)

 

All of our students are involved in both the production and the execution of the event.

 

Here are the particulars for Friday:

 

-  Seniors rehearse at the theater- 11AM – Noon

 

-  Seniors arrive for the ceremony/photographer at 4 PM

 

-  Underclassmen (PK-11) and families arrive by 4:45 PM

 

-  Full school processional begins at 5 PM

 

-The theater concession stand will be open

- ATTIRE for all students is “nice event” clothes - please, no jeans or T-shirts.  (Middle Grades students are requested to wear all black or dark clothing to enhance the special effects of their performance.)

I look forward to seeing you Friday at the Carolina Theater!
